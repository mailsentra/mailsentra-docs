# Plans and Licenses

MailSentra Pro plans are sold through Freemius. Check the current pricing page for prices, billing terms, and available license tiers.

## Site activations

Each license tier has an activation limit. Choose the tier that covers the number of sites where you will activate MailSentra Pro. Localhost activations may be treated separately according to the product's Freemius settings.

## Billing and Pro access

Monthly and annual subscriptions are billed according to the selected plan. Pro features are available when Freemius confirms an eligible Pro entitlement. Your purchase confirmation and account page show the terms that apply to your license.

Updates, support, and feature access after a subscription expires depend on the plan's configured terms. Review those terms before purchase or renewal.

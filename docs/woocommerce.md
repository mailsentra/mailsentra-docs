# WooCommerce Email Analytics

WooCommerce email activity is a Pro feature and is off until enabled in **MailSentra > Settings > Pro Features**.

When enabled, MailSentra tags future WooCommerce email attempts with their email type and reports status counts. General email logging continues to follow the selected logging mode.

Optional order analytics use a site-keyed reference to count distinct orders and aggregate product quantities. MailSentra does not store raw order IDs, product names, SKUs, or line-item details for these metrics. Review the setting and your privacy disclosures before enabling the analytics.

The WooCommerce report can show weekly or monthly activity and lets you open the matching email logs for a type and status.
